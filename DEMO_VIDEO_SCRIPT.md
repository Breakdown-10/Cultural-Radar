### 💡 Pro Tips for Recording

1. **Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps for crisp typography and smooth animations.
2. **Audio:** Use a clear microphone with minimal background noise.
3. **Cursor:** Keep cursor movements smooth and deliberate when clicking cards or charts.
4. **YouTube Settings:** Upload as **Public** or **Unlisted** (NOT Private, otherwise judges cannot view it).
